## ♡ our story

we started talking on **28th April**.

we met through a **Discord server**, completely unaware that one random meeting would eventually become something this important to me.

and then, on **5th May**, we became us.

sometimes i still think about how something as simple as talking to someone online could turn into finding a person i genuinely want beside me for the rest of my life.
